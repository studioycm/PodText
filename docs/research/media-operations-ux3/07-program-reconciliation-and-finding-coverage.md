## Literal product and operator goal

PodText must provide one trustworthy, inventory-first Media Operations
experience in which an operator can:

1. find and understand every Media record without unhealthy or unbound records
   disappearing;
2. choose an image for a podcast, episode or settings owner while keeping
   current, effective, pending and committed state distinct;
3. admit new Media from Gallery, Upload, URL, Storage or an approved provider
   path with truthful permanence and per-item outcomes;
4. inspect and perform already-authorized existing-file operations with stable
   identity, usage impact, scope, result and return continuity;
5. understand each diagnostic cause, then eventually perform only a
   reason-specific, authorized resolution with a verified result; and
6. preserve the separately gated Package 5 horizon for discovery, physical
   movement and lifecycle recovery without implying those abilities exist.

Success means the operator does not have to infer which object changes, when a
change becomes permanent, who or what is affected, whether work committed, how
to recover, or where to continue.

## Evidence language

Material claims use these evidence modes:

- **confirmed** — current source, tests, committed handoff or binding operator
  decision establishes the claim;
- **observed** — retained browser or rendered visual evidence shows the state
  at its recorded baseline;
- **operator-reported** — the operator supplied the observation, but no newer
  durable artifact was found in the retained inputs;
- **inferred** — the conclusion follows from multiple sources but has not been
  directly observed; and
- **unknown** — evidence is insufficient and the owning research task must
  answer the question.

An older screenshot remains an observation of its own baseline. It does not
override a newer committed handoff. A concept or proposal is never current
product proof.

### Authority and permanence that remain binding

- All Media is the complete Curator inventory.
- `media_attachments.media_id` is owner-attachment authority.
- Curator `path` on its configured disk is file-location authority.
- Existing Gallery selection is relationship-only and mutation-free.

  “orphaned,” or “safe to delete.”

## Jobs, operation hierarchy and surface families

| Operator job | Required information, decision and result | Current or future surface family |
|---|---|---|
| Find and understand Media | inventory completeness, identity, preview, known use, exact issue and task context | Media Library, cards, task navigation and Media Details |
| Choose for an owner | owner/slot, current direct image, effective source, candidate source, pending choice, one commit and cancel result | podcast, episode, relation-manager, workspace and settings owner entry points |
| Admit new Media | source, candidate/file identity, validation, permanence boundary, per-item receipt, failure and retry | Gallery/Upload/URL/Storage/provider intake |
| Operate on an existing file | current identity, usages, authority, proposed change, scope, confirmation, structured result, blocked state and return | Media Details and already-authorized file actions |
| Resolve a diagnosed issue | exact cause, consequence, bounded impact, eligible action or blocker, result, verification and next issue | Issue Review plus a future reason-specific resolution surface |
| Discover or govern physical lifecycle | file/row identity, containment, references, lifecycle, journal and recovery | Package 5 Files Discovery, move and lifecycle destinations |

Operations are ordered by frequency, reversibility and blast radius:

1. **Orientation and inspection** — always available and quiet.
2. **Owner choice and commit** — frequent relationship work with current,
   effective and pending state visible.
3. **Admission** — permanent creation with explicit receipts.
4. **Existing-file operations** — impact-aware, already-authorized physical
   work with result truth.
5. **Reason-specific issue resolution** — only after a cause and authority are
   selected.
6. **Package 5 discovery and lifecycle** — separately gated, highest
   consequence and never represented by dead controls.
